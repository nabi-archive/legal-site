Privacy Policy

Nabi · Bundle ID com.nabi.ios · Last updated October 21, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Nabi handles information on your iPhone. Nabi is a local archive for stray-cat care: dossiers, territories, field notes, photos, and rescue sheets. There is no Nabi account and no Nabi server that stores your archive.

By using Nabi you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, stop using the app and remove it from the device.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Nabi app distributed under the name Nabi and the bundle identifier com.nabi.ios. It does not change Apple’s policies for the App Store, iCloud, or the payment sheet Apple shows when you buy a pack.

Information stored on this device

The following stays in local storage on this device:

  • Cat dossiers you create or that are included as sample records, including name, coat, sex, age note, status, tags, territory, check-in dates, and vitals or diet notes.
  • Field notes you type, and the text produced when you dictate a note.
  • Photos you take with the camera or choose from the photo library, saved with the dossier they belong to.
  • Rescue sheets you confirm, including the summary kept in the Sheets list.
  • Your Sheet balance, the record that the starting Sheets were already granted, and switches you set in Settings, such as emergency-card and photo-save preferences.
  • A flag that remembers whether you have already passed the welcome page.

Nabi does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, or location. Territory names are labels you pick inside the app, not a map of your position.

Camera, photos, microphone, and voice

Nabi does not request camera, photo library, microphone, or speech recognition permission at launch. A system permission dialog appears only after you tap the matching action.

  • Camera. Take Photo asks for permission to use the camera so Nabi can attach a field photo to a dossier. If you allow it, the picture you capture is saved on this device. If you deny it, you can still choose an existing photo or continue without one.
  • Photo library. Choose Photo asks for permission to read photos so you can pick one image for the dossier. Saving a picture into your library, when you turn that option on, asks for permission to add a photo. Nabi reads only the image you select.
  • Microphone and voice. Speak Field Notes asks for microphone permission and speech-recognition permission so Nabi can record a short voice clip on this device and turn that audio into note text. The recording is a temporary file used for that dictation. You can stop listening, and you can always type the note instead.

If you have already denied a permission, Nabi does not show a custom permission screen. iOS will not ask again until you change that permission in the Settings app. The page explains what is unavailable and leaves the other tools usable.

How the information is used

Dossier text, photos, and voice notes are used only to show the archive, filter territories, log a check-in, preview a rescue sheet, and save a sheet you confirm. A preview does not spend a Sheet. Confirming a sheet uses one Sheet from the balance stored on this device and keeps that sheet in the Sheets tab.

Sample cats may appear the first time you enter, and again after you clear the archive and pass the welcome page. Those sample records are part of the local library. They are not an upload of your own photos.

Purchases

Sheet packs are consumable in-app purchases sold by Apple. When you tap a pack, Apple presents the payment sheet. Nabi receives the result of that transaction so it can add the matching number of Sheets to the balance on this device. Nabi does not receive your card number. Apple’s privacy policy describes what Apple collects during a purchase.

Spent Sheets are not returned by a restore action. Nabi does not offer Restore Purchases. Clearing all data sets the local balance to zero.

Third parties

Nabi does not send your photos, audio, voice notes, dossier text, or other device records to a third party for storage, advertising, or processing. There is no Nabi analytics account and no advertising identifier request.

The only third party involved in a purchase is Apple, acting as the seller of the in-app pack. If a legal page fails to load, the in-app browser shows an error and a retry control. Loading that page contacts the site that hosts this policy. It does not upload your archive.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on this device until you delete them or choose Clear All Data. Clear All Data removes cat dossiers, saved photos, generated sheet records, and the remaining Sheet balance from this device, then shows the welcome page again. Uninstalling Nabi removes the app sandbox, including the local archive, subject to the way iOS handles deleted apps.

Nabi does not keep a remote copy, so we cannot recover a cleared archive or a spent Sheet.

Children

Nabi is a care-archive tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this privacy policy changes, the updated page will be published at this same address and the date above will change. Continued use of Nabi after the update means you agree to the revised policy.
